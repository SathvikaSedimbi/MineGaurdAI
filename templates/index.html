MineGuard AI Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)

1. Low-Cost Retrofit Enclosure

Avoids the massive capital expense of autonomous conversions by retrofitting existing HEMM fleets with operator assistance displays.

2. Multi-Sensor Fused Perception

Combines RGB Camera (for class identification), Thermal (for low light and fog heat spots), and Radar (for distance and relative speed range).

3. Adaptive Environment Compensation

Dynamically switches confidence weights as visibility changes, ensuring the system remains trustworthy and never blindly depends on a single sensor.

4. Predictive Collision Algorithms

Computes closing trajectories and Time-To-Collision (TTC) to generate alerts BEFORE impacts, giving drivers reaction time.

5. Self-Aware Diagnostics

Monitors hardware health reports. Occluded lenses or connection losses trigger fail-safe cautionary speed ceilings or controlled stops.

6. Centralized Control Room Control

Pushes alerts, telemetry, paths, and explainable safety states to a single web dashboard for remote supervisor dispatch monitoring.

7. Dynamic Road Health Assessment

Uses front scanners to map potholes and surface flooding water, reducing vehicle chassis damage and tyre slips.

8. Continuity + Safety Balanced

Avoids total fleet halts. Slows high-risk units while routing others through safe bypasses, maintaining productivity.
